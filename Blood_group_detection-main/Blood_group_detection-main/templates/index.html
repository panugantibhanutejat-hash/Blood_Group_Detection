How to Use This Application

  • Place your finger on a fingerprint scanner or use a high-quality fingerprint image
  • Ensure the fingerprint image is clear with visible ridges and valleys
  • Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG (Recommended: 224x224 pixels)
  • Click "Choose Fingerprint Image" and select your fingerprint file
  • Click "Predict Blood Group" to analyze your fingerprint
  • View your predicted blood group along with confidence percentage
